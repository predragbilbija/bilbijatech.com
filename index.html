Before any change touches a live site

  • Backup first

    Every job starts with a fresh backup and, where possible, a staging copy. If anything goes wrong, there is a clean way back.

  • Fix the cause

    I look for why it broke, not just how to hide it, then verify in a clean browser with caches cleared and logs checked.

  • Document & automate

    You get a short, clear note of what was done. Anything that will happen again becomes a repeatable procedure.
